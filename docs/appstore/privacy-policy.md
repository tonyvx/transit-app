# Boston Transit privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Boston Transit ("the app") is made by Anthony Valantra. Contact: [EMAIL]

## What the app does with your data
- **Location:** the app uses your location to show your position, find nearby stations, match you to a train and plan trips from where you are. This is processed on your phone. It is not sent to the developer and is not stored on any server.
- **Preferences:** your chosen lines, routes and directions are saved on your phone only.
- **Accounts:** none. The app doesn't ask for your name, email or any personal details.
- **Analytics, advertising and tracking:** none.

## Third parties
- **MBTA:** the app requests public transit data (vehicles, predictions, schedules, alerts) from the MBTA V3 API. These requests are sent from your phone and include normal network information such as your IP address. They don't include your location.
- **Apple Maps:** address searches and walking and driving directions in the trip planner are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Those requests can include the places or coordinates you search for or route between.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal data.

## Changes
If the app starts collecting data, for example opt-in location sharing, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your permission.

This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the MBTA.
