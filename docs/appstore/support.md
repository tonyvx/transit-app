# Boston Transit support

Boston Transit is an independent app and is not affiliated with the MBTA.

**Contact:** [valantra.app@gmail.com](mailto:valantra.app@gmail.com)

To report a bug or suggest a feature from the app, tap Help & feedback on the map and choose the matching option. The email opens with prompts to help describe the issue or idea. Please do not include personal or precise location information.

## Common questions
- **No trains appear.** Open Preferences (gear icon), pick at least one line, then turn its chip on.
- **Times in the trip planner look different from the MBTA.** Planner times are scheduled, not live. Check station arrivals on the map for live predictions.
- **My location isn't used.** Allow location in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Boston Transit.
- **The trip Live Activity stops updating when the phone is locked.** Enable Allow background trip updates in Boston Transit's Preferences, then choose Always in iOS Location Services. Background location is used only during a trip you confirm.
- **I don't see a trip confirmation while the app is closed.** Allow notifications for Boston Transit so it can show a local confirmation prompt when GPS and a planned train match. If notifications are off, the prompt appears when you open the app.
- **Service alerts and official information:** see mbta.com.
